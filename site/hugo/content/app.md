<h2>What it does</h2>
<dl class="app-features">
  <div>
    <dt>The text first</dt>
    <dd>The app opens on the Hebrew text, chapter by chapter, with its labeled sections. Section headings can be hidden, and there are light, paper and dark themes.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Classes on the lines</dt>
    <dd>The classes are lined up with the text. Tap a line to hear the class explain it, from that point.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Follow along</dt>
    <dd>While a class plays, the line being explained is highlighted and kept in view.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Choose a teacher</dt>
    <dd>Classes by Rabbi Benyamin Walters and Rabbi Yossi Markel. Pick a teacher, and a class on each chapter.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>The whole library</dt>
    <dd>Every series and class, with favorites, recent classes, progress, and playback that picks up where you stopped.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Offline</dt>
    <dd>The text is built in. Download a class, or all of a chapter's classes, to listen without a connection, also in the background.</dd>
  </div>
</dl>
