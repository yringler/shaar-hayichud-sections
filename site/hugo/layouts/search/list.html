{{ .Title }}

Searches the Hebrew text, its translation, the class transcripts and their topics. A Hebrew word also finds it with prefix letters (חכמה finds ובחכמה), and key terms match across spellings and transliterations (chochmah, מידות). The transcripts are machine-made, so a term may be misspelled in them.
